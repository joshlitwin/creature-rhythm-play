The opponent shows what it will do next. Tap a creature and pick one of its moves (or Swap), then Perform: a count-in, each phrase twice, and both sides resolve on the stage. Lane 1 is your left thumb, lane 3 your right, lane 2 either. Notes joined by a bar are one press. ▲ strong / ▼ weak is the creature's type against this opponent. Calibrate

Two short tests, about 10 seconds each. Use the phone and headphones you'll play with.

1 · Sound

Tap on each click, one a second. Nothing on screen moves with the beat, so go by ear (eyes closed is fine). 4 clicks count you in, then 12 are measured.

Tap Start

2 · Sight

Silent. Tap when each dot sits inside the ring (its centre on the line, not its edge). 4 grey dots to settle in, then 12 are measured. Each tap leaves a white ring where the dot was when the tap registered.
